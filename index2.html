Our Mission

After years of research, dentist Hans Beekmans developed a compact range of supplements to support a healthy lifestyle. “We are convinced that good health begins at the cellular level. Our body’s cells require essential nutrients and minerals to generate energy and perform their functions effectively. Without optimal cell function, the foundation of your health weakens.”

Basal Basics focuses on providing the essential nutrients and minerals missing from modern diets and lifestyles. A strong foundation is the building block for a healthy body.
